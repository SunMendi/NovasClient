# PRODUCT_UI_BRIEF.md

## Product:
**Novas Defence & Maritime Portal** *(Enterprise Maritime, Defence Procurement & Shipyard Platform)*

---

## What it does:
A mission-critical B2B & B2G digital procurement and naval engineering platform. It bridges government ministries, armed forces, port authorities, and industrial conglomerates with certified global OEMs and shipyard manufacturing facilities.

Key capabilities:
- **Interactive Multi-Sector Architecture:** Dedicated deep-dives across 6 core sectors (Defence, Maritime, Heavy Industry, Geospatial Intelligence, Mission-Critical ICT, and Logistics & Supply).
- **Searchable & Filterable Equipment Catalogue:** High-density product directory (Ballistic armor, thermal/night vision, tactical comms, marine radar, sonar, UAVs, trauma care) with instant search, category filters, and featured badges.
- **Technical Specification Sheets:** Standardized technical datasheets displaying military & maritime certifications (MIL-STD-810H, NIJ Level IV, IMO/SOLAS, MED-B, ISO), dimensions, weight, operating ranges, and origin tracking.
- **Shipyard & Vessel Fleet Showcase:** Workboat, patrol craft, tugboat, and offshore vessel portfolios featuring key naval architecture specs (LOA, beam, draft, bollard pull, engine BHP, speed in knots).
- **Streamlined RFQ (Request for Quotation) Engine:** Direct inquiry workflows allowing procurement officers and fleet managers to request tender-ready proposals and custom vessel quotes.
- **Traceability & Compliance Verification:** Visible OEM provenance, audit trails, and logistics capabilities (customs clearance, hazardous freight handling, and on-site commissioning).

---

## Target users:
1. **Government & Defence Procurement Officers:** Ministry of Defence, Naval Headquarters, Air Force/Army logistics commands, Coast Guard directors, and law enforcement tenders.
2. **Commercial Maritime & Shipyard Operators:** Port authorities, offshore wind/oil support fleet managers, tugboat and pilot vessel captains, marine salvage contractors.
3. **Heavy Industry & EPC Project Directors:** Large-scale infrastructure contractors, energy and automation facility managers looking for turnkey industrial equipment.
4. **Naval Architects & Field Operations Engineers:** Technical specialists reviewing compliance sheets, payload capacities, sensor integrations, and schematics.

---

## Product personality:
- **Authoritative & Mission-Ready:** Instills immediate trust, institutional credibility, and uncompromising defense-grade compliance.
- **Precision Industrial & Data-Driven:** Clean visual hierarchy, high information density, technical spec badges, and structured data tables with zero unnecessary fluff.
- **Maritime Navy & Tactical Slate Aesthetic:** Deep oceanic blues (`#0A192F`, `#0C2340`), tactical charcoal (`#0F172A`), balanced with high-visibility signal accents (Safety Amber `#F59E0B`, Tactical Emerald `#10B981`, or Marine Blue `#0284C7`).
- **Modern, Sleek, and Fast:** Ultra-responsive filtering, smooth micro-interactions, clean glassmorphic headers, and effortless mobile/tablet accessibility.

## References & Design Benchmarks:

### 1. Damen Shipyards Group ([damen.com](https://www.damen.com/))
- **Why it fits:** The global gold standard for workboats, naval vessels, and offshore support craft.
- **Key UI/UX patterns to adopt:**
  - Vessel categorization matrix (Patrol, Tug, Crew Transfer, Offshore, Fast Ferry).
  - High-density technical specification layouts (dimensions, propulsion, tank capacities, speed graphs).
  - Clear downloadable spec sheets and direct "Inquire about this vessel" RFQ CTA.

### 2. Anduril Industries ([anduril.com](https://www.anduril.com/))
- **Why it fits:** The leading modern benchmark for defense technology and mission hardware.
- **Key UI/UX patterns to adopt:**
  - Sleek, dark, high-contrast industrial aesthetic with ultra-clean typography.
  - Video and high-res imagery showcases demonstrating hardware operational in the field.
  - Crisp spec tags and mission capability storytelling.

### 3. Kongsberg Maritime ([kongsberg.com/maritime](https://www.kongsberg.com/maritime/))
- **Why it fits:** World-renowned leader in marine electronics, hydroacoustics, radar, and naval systems.
- **Key UI/UX patterns to adopt:**
  - Clear solution taxonomy by market sector and vessel type.
  - High-trust enterprise B2B navigation and comprehensive product portfolio browsing.
  - Clean visual separation between hardware specs, software suites, and field support services.

### 4. Linear & Vercel Design Language (Modern B2B Dark/Light Polish)
- **Why it fits:** Best-in-class standard for modern web application craftsmanship (as demonstrated in the `novas-bd.lovable.app` build).
- **Key UI/UX patterns to adopt:**
  - Pill-style category filters with active counter badges.
  - Border highlight glows (`border-border/60`), subtle radial ambient light gradients, and backdrop blur headers.
  - Instantaneous client-side search and filtering with zero layout jump.

### 5. Saab Defence & Security ([saab.com](https://www.saab.com/)) / Loyd Shipyard ([loydshipyard.com](https://www.loydshipyard.com/))
- **Why it fits:** Established defence contractor and custom workboat builder references.
- **Key UI/UX patterns to adopt:**
  - Military and naval operational environment framing.
  - Direct RFQ contact touchpoints and shipyard facility capability breakdowns (drydock dimensions, slipway tonnage, steel/aluminum fabrication capacity).
